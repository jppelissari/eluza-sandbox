About ELUZA

A private travel design studio built around one moment: before commitment.

ELUZA works after the research and before the bookings, when route, sequence, pace, bases, omissions, and commitment order can still be considered as one connected decision.

Its work is professional judgment made explicit and usable—not access, inventory, or another set of options.

ELUZA is based in New York. Private consultations are by appointment, with remote engagements available across the U.S. and internationally.

Capable travelers were finding more—and trusting the whole less.

They reached the point of booking with good hotels, credible routes, and more research than ever. The uncertainty remained because no one had resolved how those individual choices would function together.

The problem had moved from discovery to decision.

ELUZA was created for that gap: the stage where someone has to take a reasoned position on the whole before individual commitments begin defining it.

The principles that keep the work independent.

Clarity before commitment.

Structural decisions are made while the trip can still respond to them—not after reservations require the rest of the route to adapt.

Design first. Bookings second. Benefits third.

The trip is judged on its own logic before booking channels or potential benefits enter the decision.

Subtraction is part of judgment.

A worthwhile place, hotel, or experience may still need to leave when it weakens the whole. More is not the default answer.

Autonomy is part of the standard.

The Blueprint must remain useful when the client books independently. Additional support is a separate choice, not a condition for making the first product usable.

JP Pelissari, Founder and steward of the standard.

JP Pelissari founded ELUZA and leads its editorial direction. He is responsible for the founding principle behind the studio and for the standard by which its recommendations, omissions, and trade-offs are judged.

The operating perspective behind that standard was formed across London, São Paulo, and New York—not as affiliations ELUZA trades on, but as the environments where the discipline behind the work took shape.

ELUZA is founder-led: its direction is set and defended by a named person, while every recommendation remains the responsibility of the studio.

Technology supports the work. Responsibility stays with ELUZA.

ELUZA uses technology for research, comparison, structured processing, and consistency. It expands what can be examined; it does not own the recommendation.

The position on what belongs, what should leave, and what must be decided first remains attributable to ELUZA.

The reasoning is visible.

The Trip Design Blueprint shows the structure of a trip and the reasoning behind its decisions in a form the client can use.

Case Studies show the same discipline applied to real, anonymized trips: the initial instinct, ELUZA’s decision, why it changed, and what the design was intended to protect.

The work is not supported by prestige claims. It is supported by reasoning that can be examined.

Design does not require surrendering execution.

The Blueprint is built for a client who intends to act independently, through their own channels and preferred suppliers. Its value remains in the structure and reasoning, regardless of who carries out the bookings.

Full Design and Signature Travel Architecture provide deeper involvement when a trip genuinely requires it. They are selected separately and do not determine whether the Blueprint can stand on its own.

If the trip is still open, the decisive work can still happen.

Begin with the path that makes the next decision clear.
