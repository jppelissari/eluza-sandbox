Fit Call

When the right starting point is not obvious.

The Fit Call is a 20-minute diagnostic conversation for trips that do not fit a responsible direct decision yet.

We listen to where the trip stands, what has already been committed, what remains unclear, and what level of involvement you expect. Then we recommend the most appropriate next step: a Trip Design Blueprint, deeper design, a different path, or no engagement yet.

Use the Fit Call when fit genuinely needs to be diagnosed.

It may be the right starting point if:

  • major flights, hotels, or other commitments are already fixed;
  • several travelers, households, or decision-makers have to be reconciled;
  • you expect hands-on booking involvement or support beyond an independent Blueprint;
  • the trip carries unusual operational or emotional complexity;
  • you are unsure whether Blueprint, Full Design, or Signature Travel Architecture matches what you need.

If your trip is still structurally open and you already understand the Blueprint, you can begin through Get Started without a Fit Call.

Twenty minutes, used for one decision.

Before the call

Choose a time online and complete a short pre-call form. It takes about five minutes and gives us the context needed to use the conversation well.

During the call

We ask focused questions about the trip, its current planning state, the decisions still open, and the kind of support you expect.

At the end

We give you one clear recommendation and explain why it fits the situation we heard. A recommendation is not an acceptance of an engagement; scope and commercial terms are confirmed separately.

A fit diagnosis, not a planning session.

The call does not provide a route, hotel recommendations, destination comparisons, booking advice, or a structure you can take away and implement. That work belongs inside a defined engagement.

The boundary keeps the conversation useful: the question here is not how to design the trip. It is whether ELUZA is the right studio, which level of involvement is proportionate, and whether now is the right moment to begin.

Share the current shape of the trip, what is already committed, and what feels most unresolved. This is not a planning questionnaire, and it is not the place to describe every preference or day.

Block 1 — Contact
If Other: Tell us more.
Block 2 — Current planning state
Name the decisions that are still genuinely open. Required only when most of the trip is already booked.
Block 3 — Destination and timing
Include the destinations you are actively considering. A shortlist is enough.
Month and year are enough if exact dates are not set.
Block 4 — Travelers
Share only what is relevant to evaluating scope—for example, a mobility consideration or a traveler configuration that changes the complexity of the trip. Do not submit medical records or diagnoses.
Block 5 — Commercial fit
A range is enough. This helps us assess realistic scope and trade-offs.
Block 6 — Need and fit
Focus on the decision or uncertainty you want the call to diagnose. A few sentences are enough.
Please do not submit payment details, passport information, medical records, or other restricted personal information.
